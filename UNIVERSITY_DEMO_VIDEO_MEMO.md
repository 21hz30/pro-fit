## Guide controls used in the video

- **Next:** advance the explanation.
- **Back:** return to the previous explanation.
- **Skip guide:** close the guide at any point.
- **How it works:** reopen help for the current page.
- **Finish:** close the last step.

Close the guide before using the page's forms. The full guide has 9 Coach steps and 8 Coachee steps; this video shows its main interactions and follows the same feature descriptions.

## Full recording script

### 1. Opening: Why I built Pro-fit

**操作：** Michael 出镜，或显示 Pro-fit 首页。开场不用点击页面，先把为什么做这个项目讲清楚。

Hello everyone. Today I'm presenting Pro-fit, a training collaboration website for student athletes and their coaches.

The common problem is that training, nutrition, recovery, and school responsibilities are often handled separately. A player may receive a workout plan, but the coach may not know how much sleep the player had, whether they are fatigued or sore, or whether schoolwork affected the session. The plan can look good on paper but still fail to match the player's real situation.

I wanted to solve this because I saw the same challenge among my own school teammates. Many of them wanted to improve, but they did not always know what to do each day, how hard they should train, or when they needed more recovery. Some players were working hard without seeing steady progress, while others were quietly dealing with poor sleep, soreness, or the pressure of school. Coaches also had limited visibility into what happened outside practice.

That made me realize that improvement is not only about adding more exercises. It is about giving each player a clear plan, helping them understand their own condition, and giving the coach enough information to make the next decision. I built Pro-fit to create that connection for my teammates and for other student athletes in similar situations. The goal is to help them train more consistently, learn better habits, avoid unnecessary overtraining, and make progress that fits their real student lives.

### 2. Entering the website: A guide for each role

**操作：** 登录过程剪掉，切到已登录的 Coach Ben。完整 Guide 从 **See who needs your support** 开始，点击 **Next** 到 **Choose a coachee and an action**，用 **Back** 返回一次，再点 **Skip guide**。录制到 Guide 高亮的真实区域，停留 2-3 秒。Guide 打开时页面表单无法操作，先关闭提示再点击页面。

When someone opens Pro-fit, they enter a workspace based on their role. A coach creates plans and reviews athletes. A player sees today's work and records what actually happened.

To make the next step clearer, I added a guide that highlights the relevant part of each page. Users can move forward with Next, go back with Back, or choose Skip guide. The full guide appears when they log in, and How it works brings back help for the page they are on.

Let me start with the coach's view.

### 3. The coach creates a plan

**操作：** 在 **Coachees** 找到 Michael 的一行，指向 **Assign / Nutrition / Review**。点击 **Assign**，进入 **Assign Workout Plan**。显示 **Load Basketball Template**、**Plan Details** 和一组动作的字段，再指向 **Save Draft / Publish Plan**。可以先用 **How it works** 查看此页的 3 个提示，依次 **Next → Next → Finish** 后再操作。演示新模板时先确认当前表单没有要保留的未保存内容。

From the roster, the coach can choose an athlete and assign a workout, plan meals, or review a check-in.

Here, the coach can start with a basketball training template and adjust it for the athlete. The week can include strength exercises, basketball skills, team play, cardio, mobility, and recovery days.

Each day can have a scheduled date, an estimated duration, sets, repetitions, rest periods, and exercise instructions. The coach can also add notes about the goal of the plan.

Save Draft keeps the work for later. After checking the dates and exercises, Publish Plan makes the workout visible to the selected athlete.

**操作：** 点击侧栏 **Nutrition**，显示 **Coachee / Scheduled date**、餐食建议与教练备注。再点 **Weekly Schedule**，选择 Michael，点击一个已有计划的日期。这里展示已有内容即可，不必为录像发布新计划。

Nutrition is planned separately, with meal ideas and coach notes for a particular day. Nutrition targets are optional.

The Weekly Schedule lets the coach check the published week and open a day to see its sessions.

### 4. The athlete follows the daily plan

**操作：** 切换到 Michael 账号，登录过程剪掉。学员 Guide 第 1 步 **Find your way around Pro-fit** 高亮四个 tab，点 **Next** 到 **Follow your coach's workout**，然后 **Skip guide**。在 **Today's Training** 中显示动作、组数或时长，点一个非篮球动作的 **Exercise Guide**，展示弹窗后关闭。

Now I'll switch to Michael's view. The guide introduces Today's Training, Weekly Plan, Training History, and My Day, so a new user knows where to start.

Today's Training focuses on the coach-assigned work for that date. It shows the exercises, duration or sets and repetitions, and movement instructions.

Exercise Guide opens movement guidance, with a link to a form tutorial or an exercise search. Basketball team activities have their own instructions on the page.

**操作：** 关闭 Guide 后，点 **Log Workout**；已有日志时按钮是 **Edit Log**。展示 **Status / Actual duration (minutes) / Training notes**。再关闭弹窗，显示 **Nutrition Plan & Log**，点 **Log a Meal**，展示 **Actual food** 与可选照片字段，再关闭。非演示记录只展示表单，不保存虚构训练或餐食。

After training, the athlete can log the workout status, actual duration, and notes. If the session was shorter than planned, they can explain why.

They can also see their coach's meal ideas and log what they ate, with an optional photo.

### 5. Looking ahead: Weekly Plan

**操作：** 点击学员 **Weekly Plan**，展示当前周的日期、训练名称和预计时长。点一个已有计划日的 **View Details**，返回当天训练；展示完再切到 **My Day**。

Weekly Plan shows the upcoming sessions and their estimated time. View Details opens the plan for a particular day. Future days are previews, which helps the athlete prepare around school and practice.

### 6. Making the plan fit: My Day

**操作：** 先在 **Today's Training** 将 **Training date** 设为当天，确认该演示日尚未提交，再打开 **My Day**。点 **How it works**，显示 **Review your day** 提示后点 **Finish**。选择 **Time available: 30 minutes / School workload: Exams / heavy homework / Energy right now: Feeling ready**，右侧会显示 **Keep it short and easy**。可编辑的演示日可以点 **Save daily priorities**，已有保存内容时按钮可能为 **Update daily priorities**，等到 **Saved for this day** 再继续。真实记录只展示已有选择。

This is My Day. The student chooses how much time they have, their school workload, and how they feel right now.

For example, I might have thirty minutes available but a heavy homework day. Pro-fit suggests a shorter, easier approach and reminds me to discuss changes with my coach.

I can save these priorities for the day. The suggestion supports planning; it does not rewrite the coach's assigned workout. My Day is private to the student, so anything the coach needs to know should also go in the daily check-in notes.

This is how the product connects training with a student's actual day.

### 7. Sending the day to the coach

**操作：** 回到 **Today's Training**，滚动到 **Additional notes for your coach**。展示备注与 **Submit Daily Check-in**。如果录制的是演示记录，先保存 My Day、训练和餐食，再提交；提交后显示只读状态。真实账号当天记录只展示入口。备注示例仅用于演示账号：`Heavy homework today. I shortened the session and would like your advice for tomorrow.`

After recording the workout and meals, the athlete adds any questions or notes for the coach. This is where they can explain that homework changed the session, or ask about fatigue or discomfort.

Submit Daily Check-in sends the day for review and makes that day's entries read-only. The coach receives the recorded activity together with the athlete's explanation.

### 8. The coach sees the full picture

**操作：** 切回 Coach Ben，关闭登录 Guide。进入 **Coachees**，显示 **Pending Reviews** 和 **Recent Activity**，点一个已有记录的 **Review**。展示 **Workout Logs / Diet logs / Coach feedback**。使用实际出现的记录；若没有照片或历史反馈，就展示空状态，不用口播声称已经存在。

Back in the coach's workspace, Pending Reviews and Recent Activity help the coach find check-ins to review.

The review brings together workout duration, training notes, meal logs, and the student's general notes. This gives the coach more context than a completion checkbox alone.

For example, if an athlete reports shortening a session because of homework or poor sleep, the coach can discuss the next session and adjust the next plan.

**操作：** 指向 **Coach feedback** 输入框和 **Submit Feedback**。只有演示记录才填写并发送示例；普通录制可以展示表单或已有反馈。示例：`Thanks for explaining your workload. Let's agree on a manageable session for tomorrow.`

The coach can send specific feedback that the athlete can return to later.

### 9. Looking back: Training History

**操作：** 切回 Michael，关闭登录 Guide，点 **Training History**。选择有记录的一天，点击 **View check-in**。有反馈时滚动到 **Coach Feedback**；若暂无反馈，停在历史记录，并说明下一次教练回复会出现在这里。切换角色不需要重放整段 Guide。

Training History lets the athlete reopen past check-ins and read any coach feedback attached to them. It creates a record of what happened and what to work on next.

### 10. How Pro-fit helps individual players improve

**操作：** 用 Today's Training、My Day 和历史记录的短镜头配合这段总结；这里的“帮助”是产品目标，不展示虚构的成效数字。

Pro-fit supports improvement by helping athletes follow a clear daily plan, find movement guidance, record real training results, keep meal logs and training notes together, and receive feedback from their coach.

My Day adds a practical question: what fits my time, school workload, and energy today?

### 11. Closing

**操作：** Michael 出镜，或停在 **My Day** 页面。最后用一句话收束到个人动机。

Pro-fit turns training into a continuous conversation. The coach creates a plan, the athlete follows and records it, the coach reviews the real situation, and the next plan becomes more personal.

That is why I built Pro-fit: to help student athletes train more intelligently, communicate more clearly, and improve in a way that fits their real lives.

### Recording notes - 不读出来

- 当前云端 My Day 已接通；睡眠、疲劳、酸痛的结构化恢复表单仍只在 local 模式。原稿中“教练直接看到完整睡眠和恢复数据”的描述已改成“通过学生备注了解情况”。当前云端录制不需要展示未接通的睡眠区块。
- My Day 的三项选择只对当前学生可见。它不会自动发送给教练，也不会自动改变已分配的训练；需要分享的情况写入 check-in 备注。
- 在当前演示项目中，完整 Guide 在登录后显示；**How it works** 重开当前页提示。**Skip guide** 可以随时结束；当前页的最后一步是 **Finish**。Coach 的全程 Guide 有 9 步，Coachee 有 8 步。
- 可以把 Guide 的开场和实际操作分段录制。Guide 打开时，关闭提示后再填写表单；每个页面切换后停 2-3 秒。
- 两个同一浏览器中的普通 tab 可能共用登录状态。录制时按顺序切换账号，并剪掉登录过程；需要两个账号同时保持登录时，用独立浏览器配置文件。
- 若页面显示 **Submitted / Reviewed / This day is read-only**，使用已有记录做展示。需演示保存和提交时，在录制前准备一条可编辑的演示记录，不清空或覆盖真实历史。
- 没有训练、反馈或照片时，口播与实际页面一致；先用已有记录，不为视频制造个人健康记录。录制画面隐藏密码和无关个人信息。
- 用这份新稿替代旧版功能口播。录制顺序不依赖发布新的训练计划，也不把样例记录当成真实使用成效。
